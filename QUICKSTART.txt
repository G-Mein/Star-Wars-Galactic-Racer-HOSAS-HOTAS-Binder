GALACTIC RACER HOSAS / HOTAS BRIDGE v1.98.3 — QUICK START
by JaffaQuake and Mars

1. Extract ZIP and double-click Install.cmd (Windows x64).
2. Input API: Auto (DirectInput first), DirectInput, or WinMM (isolated helper).
3. Select Podracer Style HOSAS or Traditional HOTAS; assign sticks and axes.
4. Twist Axis: choose independent Left and Right Twist devices/axes.
   Type: Centered (-100%..+100%, default) or Lever (0%..100%).
   Modes: Off, Fine Steering, Move Camera, Pitch, Keyboard Keys.
   Set Invert and Deadzone. Lever also offers Output Negative (0..-100%).
   Relevant mode settings appear beneath each twist.
5. Rudder Pedals remain independent and retain their established modes.
6. Standard Xbox mappings: Learn/Clear button and hat assignments in two columns.
7. Galactic Racer Specific Mappings: Pitch Up/Down and Look Left/Right/Back.
   The three Look commands continue to emit native keyboard output in-game,
   despite the equivalent Xbox directional labels in the UI.
8. Click Save settings, then Initiate Xbox Controller.
9. HidHide Setup may help if a game sees physical and virtual devices twice.

NOTE: Lever mode normally outputs 0..+100%. Check Output Negative for
0..-100% output (including negative Fine Steering and negative keyboard keys).
Invert continues to reverse physical travel independently of output polarity.
Output Negative appears only for Lever and is unchecked by default.

Settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\GalacticRacerHOSAS\hosas_config.json
