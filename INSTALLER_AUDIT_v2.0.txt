GALACTIC RACER HOSAS / HOTAS BRIDGE — v2.0 INSTALLER NOTES
=========================================================
- Installs GalacticRacerHOSAS.exe and GalacticRacerWinMMHelper.exe into Local AppData.
- Preserves hosas_config.json including prior button bindings, twist polarity
  flags and HOSAS driving-response settings (max throttle/steering, curves).

- Copies LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES, README, QUICKSTART, CHANGELOG and the
